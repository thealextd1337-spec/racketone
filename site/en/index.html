Watch connected

Built for the court

Big tap targets, haptics instead of looking, undo across sets – and no point gets lost when your phone sits in a bag by the net post.

Watch and phone as equals

Score on Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin or your phone. All devices stay in sync live – even after losing connection.

Every rule, done right

Tiebreak, match tiebreak, no-ad, golden & star point, side-out and rally scoring, setting to 30 in badminton, service changes in table tennis – singles and doubles, including serving order.

Stats that mean something

Set flow, momentum, break and set points, head-to-head against your friends and records per surface.

Heart rate along the match

Heart rate, steps and calories straight from your watch – analysed per set. Your health data stays private by default.

Play together

Friends with an account get shared matches in their app automatically. Guests can claim their profile later.

Made for sun and sweat

Dark live view with maximum contrast, water lock, and distinct vibration patterns for “us” and “them”.

How a match works

Ready in a few taps – or straight away with “Same as last time”.
