Delete your Advantly account

How to delete your account for the Advantly apps and the associated data.

In the app

  1. Open the phone app while signed in.
  2. Choose Profile → Account & privacy → Request account deletion and confirm.

Without the app

Write from your account's email address to alxpro@cakamper.at with the subject “Advantly account deletion”. We may ask you for proof that the account belongs to you.

What happens

  • Your account is hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 14 days. Until then you can withdraw the request in the app.
  • We delete your profile, email address, device and sign-in data, consents, your support messages and your matches without other accounts.
  • Matches involving other accounts remain available to them; your name and the names of your guests are replaced by “Deleted player”.
  • Logs of support and administrative actions are kept for up to one year.
  • Data on your devices remains until you uninstall the app or choose “Clear app data”.
