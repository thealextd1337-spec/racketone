Privacy policy of the Advantly apps

This policy applies to the Advantly apps for Android phones and Wear OS watches and – once available – for iPhone and Apple Watch. The German version is authoritative. The website advantly.eu is covered by its own privacy policy (German).

1. In short

  • Advantly stores your matches only locally on your phone and watch. There is currently no user account and no cloud.
  • Data is only transferred between your own devices (phone and paired watch).
  • Your heart rate is measured and shown on the watch during a match, but it is not stored in a cloud and not shared.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. As the provider, I receive none of your match or health data.

3. What data the apps process

3.1 Match data

When you score a match, the app stores the sport, rules and format, the score with every point (including corrections), timestamps and the names or labels of players that you enter yourself. This data is kept exclusively in the app's private storage on your device.

The purpose is to score, resume and display your matches. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app function you use). If you enter the names of other players, this is based on your legitimate interest in documenting your games (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); these names never leave your devices.

3.2 Transfer between your own devices

So that you can score on your phone and watch at the same time, the apps synchronise match data between your phone and your paired watch:

  • Android and Wear OS: via the Wear Data Layer API of Google Play services. Data is transferred directly over an encrypted Bluetooth connection or, if that is not available, end-to-end encrypted via Google's servers. Only the Advantly app with the same signature on your paired devices can read this data.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch (once available): via Apple's WatchConnectivity between your paired devices.

I do not operate any server for this and receive none of this data.

3.3 Heart rate and workout session on the watch

During a match, the watch app starts a workout session (Wear OS: Health Services, Apple Watch: workout session). This keeps the app running while the display is off, so the score is not lost. If you allow it, the app also shows your current heart rate.

  • Heart rate is read only while a match session is running, used only for display on the watch and not stored permanently by Advantly.
  • It is not uploaded to a cloud, not shared with me or third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Advantly currently writes nothing to Health Connect, Apple Health or other health stores.
  • The measurement itself is performed by your watch's sensors and system services (Google or Samsung on Wear OS, Apple on watchOS) under their own privacy terms.

Heart rate is health data (Art. 9 GDPR). It is only processed if you grant the permission and start a match; the legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can revoke the permission at any time in the watch settings – scoring keeps working, just without the heart rate display.

3.4 What Advantly does not do

The apps contain no ads, no tracking or analytics tools and do not use the advertising ID. The current versions have no internet permission, there is no user account and no location access. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Permissions and their purpose

The watch app (Wear OS) only asks for permissions it needs for scoring with a workout session. You can deny or revoke all of them.

  • Heart rate / body sensors (health.READ_HEART_RATE, up to Wear OS 5 BODY_SENSORS): showing your heart rate during the match.
  • Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): steps recorded by the watch's system services during the workout session.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): an ongoing-match notice that takes you back into the app.
  • “Health” foreground service and keeping the device awake: so the match keeps running while the display is off.
  • Vibration (phone and watch): haptic confirmation of each point.

5. Retention and deletion

Match data stays on your devices until you delete it. You can delete individual matches in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings removes all data stored locally by the app on that device. Heart rate is not stored and therefore does not need to be deleted.

If you have enabled Android backup to your Google account, the system may include the phone app's match data in that backup. Google manages this backup for you; I have no access to it. You can control and delete it in the Android settings under “Backup”.

6. Google Play and App Store

When you download Advantly from Google Play (or later Apple's App Store), the store operator processes data under its own responsibility. If you have agreed on your device to share crash and usage data with Google or Apple, I can see aggregated, non-personal crash statistics in the respective developer console.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Since your data is currently only stored on your devices, you can exercise most of these rights directly yourself (view, edit and delete in the app, revoke permissions). For questions, please use the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at. You may also contact the supervisory authority of your place of residence.

8. Minimum age

Advantly is intended for people aged 16 and over.

9. Changes

Advantly is under development. With user accounts, friends and cloud sync (phase 2) and the storage and analysis of health data (phase 3), this policy will be extended before those features become available. Even then, health data will only be stored with your separate, explicit consent.
