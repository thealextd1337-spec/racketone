Privacy policy of the Advantly apps

This policy applies to the Advantly apps for Android phones and Wear OS watches and – once available – for iPhone and Apple Watch. The German version is authoritative. The website advantly.eu is covered by its own privacy policy (German).

1. In short

  • The phone app requires an account. You create it on first launch; the watch needs no sign-in of its own.
  • The phone app backs up your matches – including those scored on the watch – automatically to our cloud (data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany) so you can use and restore them on several devices. Scoring also works offline; the backup runs as soon as you are online. You only share matches with friends if you switch it on – per match or as the default for new matches.
  • Your heart rate is measured on the watch and, depending on your setting, also shown on your phone. It is not sent to the cloud and not stored.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. Your data is not sold.
  • You can view and export your account data in the app at any time and request deletion of your account.

3. Keeping score on your devices

3.1 Match data on your devices

When you keep score, the app stores the sport, rules and format, the score with every point (including corrections), timestamps, an optional venue and the names or labels of the players you enter yourself. This data is kept in the app's private storage on your devices and is also backed up to your account (section 4).

The purpose is to keep score, resume and display your matches. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app function you use). If you enter names of other players, this is based on your legitimate interest in recording your games (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.2 Transfer between your own devices

So that you can keep score on phone and watch at the same time, the apps exchange match data, connection and version status and, during a match, heart rate and steps between your phone and your paired watch:

  • Android and Wear OS: via the Wear Data Layer API of Google Play services. The transfer runs directly over an encrypted Bluetooth connection or, if that is unavailable, end-to-end encrypted through Google servers. Only the Advantly app with the same signature on your paired devices can read this data.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch (once available): via Apple's WatchConnectivity between your paired devices.

The watch app itself has no internet access. Data only reaches the cloud through the phone app (section 4).

3.3 Heart rate and workout session on the watch

During a match, the watch app starts a workout session (Wear OS: Health Services, Apple Watch: workout session). This keeps the app running with the display off so the score is not lost. If you allow it, the app shows your current heart rate and steps.

  • Under “Heart rate & steps” you choose whether the values are shown only on the watch, also on your paired phone, or not at all.
  • Advantly does not store heart rate or steps permanently, does not send them to the cloud, does not pass them on to us or third parties and does not use them for advertising.
  • Advantly currently writes nothing to Health Connect, Apple Health or any other health store.
  • The measurement itself is performed by your watch's sensors and system services (Google or Samsung on Wear OS, Apple on watchOS) under their own privacy terms.

Heart rate is health data (Art. 9 GDPR). It is only processed if you grant the permission and start a match; the legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can revoke the permission at any time in the watch settings – scoring keeps working, just without the heart rate display.

4. Account and cloud backup

The phone app can only be used with an account. You create it when you first launch the phone app; the watch app has no sign-in of its own. Before you use the app, you accept the terms of use and confirm that you have taken note of this privacy policy in the version shown; we store the document, version, language and time. When a new version is released, the app asks again before you continue scoring. If you decline, you can sign out; data export and account deletion remain available, and you can use the app again once you confirm. Data that has already been backed up is kept until your account is deleted.

Matches scored on this phone or your paired watch before you signed in are automatically assigned to your account and backed up when you sign in. After signing out or requesting deletion, the sign-in screen appears again.

4.1 Sign-in and age check

  • Sign-in: with your email address and a one-time code that we send you by email. If you choose “Sign in with Google” instead, we receive your email address and an account identifier from Google.
  • Age check: you enter your country and date of birth. The date of birth is only checked and not stored. We store the country, the applicable age limit and the time of confirmation. To prevent abuse, we briefly store an irreversible hash of your IP address and of the check (deleted after 25 hours at the latest).
  • Profile: a display name (alias) of your choice, a username, an automatically generated friend code and your language setting.

4.2 What is sent to the cloud

  • Your matches with all points and corrections, timestamps, optional venue and the names of the players you entered (including names of guests without their own account).
  • Technical identifiers of your installations and devices (randomly generated IDs, device type and platform), so that data is only accepted from your own devices and a lost device can be blocked through support.
  • Your consents with document, version, language and time.

The purpose is to back up, restore and synchronise your matches between your devices. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Who else can see your matches is up to you (section 4.3).

Your “Heart rate & steps” setting (share, only me, off) is stored with your account so that it applies the same way on all your devices. The heart rate and step values themselves are still not uploaded to the cloud.

4.3 Friends and shared matches

  • Finding friends: others can only find you by your exact username or your friend code and send you a friend request. Your display name (alias) and username are shown. You can accept or decline requests, remove friends and block accounts; blocked accounts can no longer find or contact you.
  • Sharing matches: for each match you decide whether your friends can see it (“Share with friends”, off by default; you can change the default under Profile). Friends then see a limited view with sport, date, score, match details such as surface or indoor/outdoor and the participants’ display names – without device details and without heart rate or steps. If a player added to the match shares it, their friends see this view too. You can hide a match or stop sharing at any time.
  • Friends as players: if you add a friend to a match, the match is linked to their account and also appears in their history; players added to a match see the display names of all participants. With “I wasn’t there” they can object to the link; the match then disappears from their view.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the friends and sharing features you use). When you add friends to matches, this is additionally based on your legitimate interest in recording your games together (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4.4 Security, logs and abuse prevention

All connections are encrypted with TLS. For every request, our service providers process technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time and app identifier in order to provide the service and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Supabase keeps these access logs for one day, Cloudflare keeps security events for up to 31 days, and Resend keeps sending logs and email content for 30 days.

5. Messages to support

Under “Feedback & help” you can send us messages – with an account or as a guest. We store the category, title and text, an optional contact email address and, for messages sent with an account, the sending device. Only if you tick the box do you also send the technical details shown (app version, operating system, device model, screen size and font size). For guest messages we store a hash of your IP address to prevent abuse.

The purpose is to handle your request and improve the app (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete resolved messages 180 days after closing them. Logs of support and administrative actions are kept for one year.

6. Recipients and service providers

We do not pass your data on to third parties and do not sell it. We use the following service providers as processors (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore): database, sign-in and file storage. The data is stored in an Amazon Web Services data centre in Frankfurt am Main. EU standard contractual clauses apply to support and operations access from third countries.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): upstream network for protection and delivery and operation of our administration interface.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA): sending the emails with sign-in codes. Emails are sent from Ireland; Resend stores sending logs and email data in the USA.

Cloudflare and Resend are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision and, in the alternative, on EU standard contractual clauses. The data processing agreements are part of each provider's terms.

For Google sign-in and the exchange between phone and watch you use Google services under Google's own terms.

7. Retention and deletion

  • On your devices: delete individual matches in the phone app under History → match → Delete. Remove all local data by uninstalling the app on phone and watch or choosing “Clear app data”.
  • Account and cloud: the data is kept as long as your account exists. Under Profile → Account & privacy you can request deletion; your account is hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 14 days. You can withdraw the request within this period. Without the app, you can request deletion via advantly.eu/en/delete-account.html.
  • Matches involving other accounts remain available to them; your name and the names of guests you created are replaced by “Deleted player”. Matches without other accounts are deleted.
  • We delete unfinished registrations after 24 hours and data exports after 24 hours.
  • Backups: there are currently no backup copies of the cloud data at the hosting provider. Deleted data is permanently removed once deletion is complete. Please also keep important matches via the data export.

If you have enabled Android backup in your Google account, the system may include the phone app's data in that backup. Google manages this backup for you; we have no access to it.

8. Permissions and their purpose

  • Internet (phone only): account, cloud backup and messages to support.
  • Heart rate / body sensors (watch; health.READ_HEART_RATE, up to Wear OS 5 BODY_SENSORS): showing your heart rate during the match.
  • Physical activity (watch; ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): counting steps during the workout session.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): notice of the ongoing match that takes you back into the app.
  • Foreground service “health” and keeping the screen on (watch): so the match continues with the display off.
  • Vibration (phone and watch): tactile confirmation of every point.

9. What Advantly does not do

The apps contain no ads, no tracking or analytics tools and use neither an advertising ID nor your location. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Google Play and App Store

When you download Advantly from Google Play (or later Apple's App Store), the store operator processes data under its own responsibility. If you have agreed on your device to share crash and usage data with Google or Apple, we see aggregated crash statistics in the respective developer console.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Much of this you can do directly in the app: view and export your account data under Profile → Account & privacy, request deletion and revoke permissions; we block a device through support on request. For anything else, write to the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at. You may also contact the supervisory authority of your place of residence.

12. Minimum age

Because the phone app requires an account, Advantly is intended for people aged 14 and over in Austria and 16 and over in all other countries, except countries where Advantly is not offered because of embargoes. Your data is processed as described in this policy regardless of your country of residence.

13. Changes

Advantly is under development. We extend this policy before new features such as live spectators or storing health data become available. For material changes we ask for your consent again in the app. Health data will only be stored with your separate, explicit consent.
